## Research & Learn

### Professional vs. unprofessional behaviour

Professional behaviour means being respectful, prepared, and finishing work on time. Unprofessional behaviour includes being rude, unprepared, or ignoring feedback.

### Respectful communication

Respectful communication means being polite, listening to others, and replying clearly.

### Giving and receiving feedback

Give feedback kindly and focus on the work. Accept feedback and use it to improve.

### Working with supervisors

I learned that I should prepare for meetings, take notes, ask questions, give updates, and take initiative.

---

## Reflection

I think good teamwork happens when everyone communicates clearly and helps each other.

I will use clear and respectful communication and ask questions when I am unsure.

I can help create a positive workplace by being kind, respectful, and completing my work on time.

Before meetings, I will prepare my questions and take notes.

I will be proactive by looking for solutions, giving updates, and asking for help when needed.

If I need a response, I will send a polite follow-up message. If it is urgent, I will use another way to contact my supervisor.

I will not ignore feedback, be disrespectful, or come to meetings unprepared.
