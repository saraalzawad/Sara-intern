## Research & Learn

### 1. Common cyber security threats
Common threats include phishing emails, malware, weak passwords, and fake websites.

### 2. Best practices
Keep devices updated, use strong passwords, enable 2FA, and lock computer when away.

### 3. Why lock your computer?
It protects company information and stops other people from accessing work.

### 4. How to handle phishing?
Do not click suspicious links or open unknown attachments. Report if needed.

### 5. Strong passwords and password manager
Use long, unique passwords. A password manager helps store them safely.

### 6. Why use 2FA?
2FA adds extra security. should enable it on all important accounts.
