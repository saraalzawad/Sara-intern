I want to compare Focus Bear with an application that does not appear on the list. I have been using an application called Forest since high school and think that there are some features in it which will improve Focus Bear. The main feature I like is the option to invite your friends and see who has accomplished the highest focus hours. It gives a sense of healthy competition amongst friends. For instance, during my high school final exams, me and my friends used to study for hours and see who had accomplished the highest number of focus sessions. We could join the same focus session online and complete it as well. Although Focus Bear is better as it is available on multiple platforms whereas Forest is only available on iPhones. Another reason is that I like Focus Bear as it incorporates several helpful features in one application, such as a to-do list, daily planner, routine planner, and focus options, therefore, I don't have to install several applications for these tasks.
