## Research & Learn

### Who are considered vulnerable populations, and what challenges might they face in digital spaces?
Vulnerable populations include people with disabilities, people with ADHD or Autism, older adults, and people who are not good with technology. They may find apps and websites hard to use because they are confusing or have too much information.

### What ethical considerations are important when designing or working with neurodivergent individuals?
We should be respectful, communicate clearly, protect people's privacy, and make sure everyone feels included.

### How can you make interactions and content more accessible for people with ADHD or Autism?
I can use simple words, clear instructions, and break big tasks into small steps. I should also avoid making the screen too busy.

### How can we support neurodivergent team members in a professional setting?
We should communicate clearly, be patient, respect different ways of working, and offer help when needed.

## Task

I read about a person with ADHD who said that too much text and too many choices made it hard to focus. They liked simple layouts and step-by-step instructions. Because of this, I think Focus Bear can be improved by using shorter instructions and breaking big tasks into smaller steps. If a user was having difficulty, I would say, "Thank you for telling me. Let's take it one step at a time. If you need help, I am happy to explain it more clearly."
